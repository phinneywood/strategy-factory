## 1. Problem and decision

Status: In progress

**User proposal:** Start a neighborhood home-maintenance reminder service, assuming people will pay $15/month because they forget maintenance. Source: user's request, 2026-09-21.

**Decision to develop:** Is there a recurring maintenance problem for an identifiable group of neighbors that a paid reminder service can solve?

**Evidence supplied:** The idea and proposed price. No customer examples, interviews, payments or observed maintenance outcomes have been supplied.

**Assumptions:** Forgetting is a material cause of missed maintenance; reminders will prompt action; customers value the result enough to pay $15/month; value recurs enough to retain subscribers.

**Strong alternative explanation:** People may already remember the work but lack time, money, knowledge or a trusted provider. A reminder alone would leave those barriers unresolved. This is a competing hypothesis, not an observed fact.

Decision-maker: user. Available budget: $100; available time: one hour per week. Desired business outcome, target customer and neighborhood: not yet established.
