# sharpen-idea

Use a Grill-Me-style interview, one consequential question at a time.

Establish:
- problem and who experiences it
- evidence the problem exists
- desired change in outcome
- decision-maker or beneficiary
- constraints and non-goals
- proposed mechanism, if any
- assumptions that must be true

Do not let a proposed solution stand in for the problem.

Finish with:
1. problem statement
2. provisional thesis
3. critical assumptions
4. strongest plausible alternative explanation
5. next question the workflow should answer
