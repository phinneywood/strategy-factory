# sharpen-idea

Own Stage 1: **Problem and decision**.

## Entry

Stage 1 has no predecessor. In an active case it must be the first stage worked. Do not work later-stage content while this gate is open.

## Method

Adapt [Matt Pocock's grilling](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/blob/main/docs/productivity/grilling.md): explore decisions in dependency order, surface hidden assumptions, and resolve facts independently.

Use one consequential question at a time. Establish the problem, affected people, desired outcome, decision to be made, decision authority, constraints, and material assumptions. Challenge the proposed mechanism and its strongest reasonable alternative explanation or approach. Stop when the decision is clear enough to determine what evidence should be gathered next.

## Section contract

Update only Section 1 plus the case opening fields and any status changes caused by this work. Keep the section compact: what is known, what it means, and what remains open.

## Exit criteria

Mark Stage 1 `Complete` only when all are true:

- The problem and affected people are concrete enough to investigate.
- The decision to be made is explicit.
- The decision owner or authority is identified, or the case records why formal authority is not material.
- Desired outcome and material constraints are explicit.
- The key assumptions and unknowns are visible.
- The proposed mechanism has been challenged against a credible alternative explanation or approach.
- No unresolved Stage 1 question would materially change what evidence Stage 2 should gather.

If any criterion fails, remain `In progress` and ask or investigate the single most consequential gap.

## Completion

Record one short `**Gate:** Passed — ...` rationale, save and verify the case, then allow the coordinator to unlock Stage 2. Do not unlock Stage 2 before verified persistence.
