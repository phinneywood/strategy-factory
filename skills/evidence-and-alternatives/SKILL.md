# evidence-and-alternatives

Own Stage 2: **Evidence and alternatives**.

## Entry

Read the current Strategy Factory case first. If none exists, route to Stage 1 rather than creating a Stage 2-only case. Stage 1 must be `Complete`. Otherwise do not perform Stage 2 research; route to Stage 1.

## Method

Find decision-relevant evidence using primary sources where practical. Combine three activities because they serve one decision gate:

1. **Established methods:** identify the few frameworks, standards, or operating patterns that fit the problem.
2. **Implemented prior art:** find concrete examples, repositories, products, programs, or case studies; record context, observed results, and transfer limits.
3. **Alternatives:** compare the proposed approach with credible options such as build, adapt/buy, process change, and defer/stop.

Use proportionate options appraisal adapted from [HM Treasury's Green Book](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-green-book-appraisal-and-evaluation-in-central-government/the-green-book-2026). Prefer adapting proven patterns over inventing a framework. Treat assumptions as assumptions, not evidence.

## Section contract

Section 2 should answer: what evidence changes the Stage 1 decision, what alternatives survive scrutiny, and which direction currently deserves further investment. Keep research notes subordinate to those conclusions.

## Exit criteria

Mark Stage 2 `Complete` only when all are true:

- The main factual claims needed to assess the proposed mechanism have credible evidence or are explicitly identified as unresolved.
- Relevant established methods have been assessed for fit rather than merely listed.
- At least one implemented analogue has been examined, or the case records that a reasonable search found none.
- Credible alternatives have been compared, including a stop/defer path when material.
- Evidence, assumptions, and transfer limits are distinguished.
- A preferred direction is selected, including stop/defer when appropriate.
- No unresolved Stage 2 evidence gap would make Stage 3 value analysis premature.

If evidence remains too weak to select a direction, remain `In progress` and pursue the highest-value gap.

## Completion

Record one short `**Gate:** Passed — ...` rationale, save and verify, then allow Stage 3 to unlock.
