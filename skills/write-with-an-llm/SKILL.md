# write-with-an-llm

Own Stage 6: **Working draft and critique**.

## Entry

Stages 1–5 must be `Complete`. Otherwise do not draft the final narrative; route to the earliest incomplete predecessor.

## Method

Adapt [Thomas Ptacek's How To Write With An LLM](https://sockpuppet.org/blog/2026/09/17/how-to-write-with-an-llm/): by default, the user writes or dictates and the model identifies specific problems without praise or replacement prose. Work in useful passes over argument, order, clarity, unsupported claims, and repetition.

If the user explicitly requests drafting or rewriting, comply and label the switch `Assisted drafting`. Do not call assisted drafting Ptacek's method.

## Section contract

Preserve user-authored drafts unless edits are requested. Keep the working text and critique in Section 6; do not silently revise strategy established upstream.

## Exit criteria

Mark Stage 6 `Complete` only when all are true:

- A complete draft exists in the chosen format.
- The decision or ask is explicit.
- Material claims are consistent with the case evidence and do not contradict completed upstream stages.
- Material caveats and uncertainty are preserved.
- The draft has received at least one substantive critique pass.
- The user accepts the draft as complete enough for its intended use.

## Completion

Record one short `**Gate:** Passed — ...` rationale, save and verify, then allow Stage 7 to unlock.
