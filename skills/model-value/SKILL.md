# model-value

Own Stage 3: **Value and adoption**.

## Entry

Read the current Strategy Factory case first. If none exists, route to Stage 1. Stages 1 and 2 must both be `Complete`. Otherwise do not perform Stage 3 analysis; route to the earliest incomplete predecessor.

## Method

Adapt the Green Book's Five Case Model proportionately: strategic fit, comparative value, commercial feasibility, affordability, and delivery. Trace intervention → behavior → outcome → value.

Record baselines, ranges, evidence strength, costs, displaced work, adoption friction, incentives, owners, and the commitment needed now. Separate released capacity from cash savings and avoid double-counting. Compare against the strongest viable alternative, including doing less or stopping.

Use Amazon Working Backwards only when it clarifies the proposed customer or user experience; it is optional and does not replace the value case.

## Section contract

Section 3 should make the economic and adoption logic legible without turning into a full business plan. Preserve uncertainty beside the claim it qualifies.

## Exit criteria

Mark Stage 3 `Complete` only when all are true:

- Strategic fit and the intervention-to-value chain are explicit.
- Material benefits and costs have a baseline, range, or justified qualitative treatment.
- Opportunity cost and the strongest alternative are included.
- Adoption friction, incentives, owners, and delivery feasibility are addressed.
- Released capacity is not mislabeled as cash savings.
- The required commitment now is explicit, including a no-go or defer decision if that is the preferred direction.
- The material uncertainties that could still reverse the decision are explicit inputs to Stage 4 validation.

If the case cannot yet support these judgments, remain `In progress`.

## Completion

Record one short `**Gate:** Passed — ...` rationale, save and verify, then allow Stage 4 to unlock.
