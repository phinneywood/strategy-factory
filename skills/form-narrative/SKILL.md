# form-narrative

Own Stage 5: **Narrative structure**.

## Entry

Stages 1–4 must be `Complete`. Otherwise do not structure the pitch or memo; route to the earliest incomplete predecessor.

## Method

Choose one format based on audience and decision.

For a presentation, adapt [Sumeet Gayathri Moghe's Nail your narrative](https://martinfowler.com/articles/never-send-slides/nail-your-narrative.html): central idea and stakes → audience and takeaways → fitting storyline → explain without visuals → storyboard only if useful.

For a decision memo, adapt [Amazon's narrative practice](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/2017-letter-to-shareholders): a self-contained prose argument that supports informed discussion without a presenter.

Do not use narrative structure to conceal unresolved weaknesses from Stages 1–4.

## Section contract

Section 5 contains the audience, decision or ask, central idea, stakes, chosen format, and ordered argument. Keep prose drafting in Stage 6.

## Exit criteria

Mark Stage 5 `Complete` only when all are true:

- The audience and decision or ask are explicit.
- The central idea and stakes are explicit.
- One format is selected for a reason.
- The storyline covers the decision, evidence, alternatives, value, adoption, risks, and commitment in a coherent order.
- Material uncertainty and counterarguments remain visible.
- The outline is complete enough that drafting can begin without inventing missing strategy.

## Completion

Record one short `**Gate:** Passed — ...` rationale, save and verify, then allow Stage 6 to unlock.
