# form-narrative

Do not begin with slides.

Establish:
- audience and decision-maker
- decision requested
- central idea
- why now
- relevant problem/evidence
- considered alternatives
- proposed mechanism
- expected value and uncertainty
- risks and mitigations
- evidence still to be learned
- specific commitment requested

Build the storyline before choosing presentation format. Preserve important uncertainty; do not upgrade assumptions into facts for rhetorical strength.

The output may become a memo, one-pager, verbal pitch, or slide storyline depending on the user's need.
