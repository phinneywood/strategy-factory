# review-outcome

Own Stage 7: **Outcome and learning**.

## Entry

Read the current Strategy Factory case first. If none exists, route to Stage 1. Stages 1–6 must be `Complete`. Real outcome evidence must exist. If implementation has not produced observable results yet, Stage 7 may remain `In progress` but must not invent outcomes.

## Method

Adapt [HM Treasury's Magenta Book](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/the-magenta-book/magenta-book-central-government-guidance-on-evaluation-html): compare intended mechanisms, actual adoption, costs, and outcomes; examine attribution and credible alternative explanations.

Use Stage 4's original predictions and thresholds. Separate outcome quality from decision quality.

## Section contract

Section 7 records expected versus observed results, adoption, costs, attribution limits, what to continue/change/stop, and the reusable lesson. Update the case opening's current view and next move.

## Exit criteria

Mark Stage 7 `Complete` only when all are true:

- Original forecasts and thresholds are compared with observed results.
- Actual adoption and material costs are addressed.
- Attribution and plausible alternative explanations are considered.
- Outcome quality is distinguished from the quality of the original decision process.
- A continue, change, or stop conclusion is recorded.
- At least one reusable lesson is explicit.
- The case's current view reflects what was learned.

## Completion

Record one short `**Gate:** Passed — ...` rationale and save and verify the final case state.
