# Review outcome

Retrieve the original case checkpoint before interpreting results.

Compare:
- predicted outcome vs observed outcome
- leading indicators vs actual behavior
- assumptions that held
- assumptions that failed
- unexpected effects
- adoption reality
- realized cost/value where measurable

Separate outcome variance from reasoning quality: a good decision can have a bad outcome and vice versa.

End with:
1. what changed in the case
2. what should happen next
3. one or two reusable lessons about the user's judgment or decision process
